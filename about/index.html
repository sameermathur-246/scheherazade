About Scheherazade

Scheherazade is a South African music organisation and arts collective dedicated to creating performances, publications and cultural projects that explore the stories, traditions and artistic heritage of people and cultures.

Working across music, dance, visual art and literature, the collective brings artists together to develop projects that encourage cultural exchange.
